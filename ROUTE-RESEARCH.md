# Jess's Birthday Adventure: route and research
Updated November 3, 2026. Birthday is Monday, November 16, 2026.

## The route: South Main to the north-end park

We are **NOT making candles or booking a workshop**. Start at Ruby Mae’s at the south end, continue to Wonderland, shop for ready-made candles at Crafty Mountain Candle Co. on Brucken Strasse, browse downtown, pause by the river before dark, enjoy birthday dinner at Bangkok Haus on Edelweiss Strasse, collect sweets if the shop is open, and finish **around Unicoi Hill Park** at the north end. This remains a south-to-north adventure overall, with side detours to the candle shop, the river, and Bangkok Haus on Edelweiss Strasse; it is not a perfectly one-way walk on every block.

## Nine sequential chapters

1. **The Gathering — Ruby Mae’s**, 8160 S Main St Unit A-4. Start the quest with friends and Jess’s keepsake. Confirm the friend meet-up time.
2. **The Cabinet of Curious Things — Wonderland Antiques & Curiosities**, 8251 S Main St. Head north through South Main. Monday opening hours have not been confirmed reliably; if closed, the outdoor observation clue can still be completed.
3. **The Candlekeeper’s Cabinet — Crafty Mountain Candle Co.**, 705 Brucken Strasse Building 101. Shopping only: ready-made candles, wax melts, scents. No class, reservation, pouring, cooling or pickup plan. Official site lists Monday 11:30 AM–3 PM. If arrival will be after closing, keep the stop flexible and look for other open candle shops rather than breaking the one-way route.
4. **The Glassblowing Shop — Fire & Colored Glass**, 8600 N Main St, Unit 1. Browse the colorful glasswork and collect its own Journal memory, photo, achievement and jar. Confirm Monday hours.
5. **ZuZu’s Petals Rock Shop — The Cabinet of Curious Stones**, White Horse Square, 8660 N Main St. Independently explore the rocks, crystals and minerals; earn a second Journal memory, photo, achievement and jar before continuing north. Confirm Monday hours.
6. **The Chattahoochee Pause**, public river access near River Street/Fussen Platz. A short daylight side excursion; on November 16 sunset is approximately 5:29 PM EST. No unlit or closed river/forest trails.
7. **Birthday Dinner — Bangkok Haus**, 689 Edelweiss Strasse. Jess chose Thai and sushi here instead of Heidelberg. Its published Monday hours are 11:30 AM–9 PM, but confirm dining hours and seating. The restaurant is a detour along Edelweiss Strasse rather than on North Main. Its online menu and local visitors bureau give 689, while some third-party pages say 707. Use its business name for navigation.
8. **The Sweet Interlude — Hansel & Gretel Candy Kitchen**, 8651 N Main St. Grab a sweet treasure if open. The official candy shop website lists conflicting Monday closing times (6 PM and 8 PM on different sections), so verify in advance; if closed, complete the exterior clue without entering.
9. **The Moonlit Finale — Unicoi Hill Park AREA**, North Main St / Pete’s Park Rd, approximate map coordinates 34.70286, -83.73157. The park/gazebo's nighttime access is **not confirmed**. The finale works on nearby public streets if the park itself is closed. This is the city park, not Unicoi State Park.
